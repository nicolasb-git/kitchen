### Ingredients
- [ ] 16 WRAPS
- [ ] 1 oignon
- [ ] 500 g of vegetarian haché
- [ ] 16 slices of cheddar
- [ ] Old el Paso sauce (at will)
- [ ] Gratted cheese (at will)
- [ ] maïs (at will)
- [ ] red beans

### Instructions

1. **Cook the oignon**  
   Slice the oignon, cook it with vegetal oil and a part of the sauce

2. **Cook the haché**  
   Cook the haché with the oignons

3. **Arrange in the Baking Dish**  
   Roll each wrap with the oignon/haché mix + slice of cheddar + red beans + maïs.
   Dispose in the gratin plate.
   Do it again until there is no more left

4. **Add gratted cheese onto of the preparation**
   Add also the rest of the sauce

6. **Bake**  
   Bake in the oven at 200 °C (390 °F) for 30–35 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden.
